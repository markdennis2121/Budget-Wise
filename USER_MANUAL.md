## 1. First-Time Launch & Security

When you launch Penny for the first time, the application will guide you through two critical onboarding and security steps:

### 📜 Terms & Conditions
* **Purpose:** Penny is a highly professional, offline-first application. You will be prompted with a formal scrollable legal declaration confirming that your transaction history and account balances are stored strictly on your local device.
* **Action:** Read the agreement fully and tap **"I Accept and Agree"** to unlock the ledger.

### 🔑 6-Digit PIN Security
* **Purpose:** To prevent unauthorized local access to your budget ledger.
* **Configuration:** 
  1. On your first entry, establish a secure **6-digit lock PIN**.
  2. Every subsequent startup will prompt you for this PIN before displaying your dashboard.
  3. All data remains encrypted within your local storage.

## 2. Core Budgeting Concepts

Penny operates on the classic, highly effective **Envelope Budgeting** methodology:

* **Ready to Assign:** This represents your current unallocated cash pool (e.g. recent income that hasn't been assigned to a budget category yet).
* **Total Actual Money:** The sum of all your unassigned cash plus the balances across all your wallets and bank accounts.
* **Assigning Money:** Tap **"Assign"** on the main dashboard to distribute your unassigned income among your envelopes.

## 3. Managing Accounts & Wallets

Track your cash flow across different physical or digital wallets (e.g. Cash, GCash, PayMaya, Bank Accounts).

* **Adding an Account:**
  1. Under the **"Wallets & Accounts"** section on your Home Screen, tap **"Add Account"**.
  2. Enter a custom name and input your current starting balance.
  3. Select your wallet provider type for custom color branding.
* **Editing an Account:** Tap the small pencil icon on any wallet card to edit its name or adjust the current balance.

## 4. Using Envelope Allocations

Envelopes are your spending category limits (e.g., Food, Transport, Rent, Savings).

### 🏷️ Clean Dynamic Icon Mapping
Penny automatically scans your envelope names to map clean, minimal vector icons—no messy emojis, keeping your ledger strictly professional:
* **Rent / Housing / Rent:** Dynamic `home` icon.
* **Food / Dining / Groceries:** Dynamic `restaurant` icon.
* **Transport / Gas / Commute:** Dynamic `directions-car` icon.
* **Bills / Utilities / Internet:** Dynamic `receipt` icon.
* **Savings / Investments:** Dynamic `savings` icon.
* **Education / School / Books:** Dynamic `school` icon.
* **Custom/Other:** Dynamically labeled with a clean minimal arrow tag.

## 5. Interactive Analytics & Doughnut Chart

Navigate to the **Analytics** tab to view your spending breakdown.

* **Segment Interaction:** Tap any slice on the interactive SVG Doughnut Chart to view its specific envelope category and the exact amount spent.
* **Quick Toggle:** Tap on any category listed in the legend below to highlight its respective slice instantly.
* **Reset View:** Tap the highlighted center summary card to return to your total monthly spending view.

## 6. Automated Notification System

Penny runs automatic system background reminders to keep your logging disciplined:

### ⏰ Automated Schedules
* **Daily Budget Check (8:00 PM):** A subtle, professional nudge reminding you to record your cash flow for the day:
  > *"Have you logged your transactions today? Keep your budget accurate."*
* **Upcoming Bill Alerts (9:00 AM):** Automatically triggers **1 day before** any scheduled bill's due date to avoid overdue charges.
  > *"Upcoming Bill: [Name] - Your bill of ₱[Amount] is due tomorrow."*

### ⚙️ Diagnostic Center
Tap the **Notification Bell** 🔔 in the top-right corner of the Home screen to view scheduled alerts or tap **"Run Notification Diagnostics"** to immediately test and verify native push pop-ups.

## 7. Offline Data & Privacy Policy

* **100% Offline-First:** Penny does not upload your transactional ledgers, account numbers, PINs, or budgets to any cloud servers. 
* **Data Security:** All records are saved securely inside your device's persistent storage. Clearing the application data or uninstalling the app will wipe the local database—remember to export or record your details when changing devices!
