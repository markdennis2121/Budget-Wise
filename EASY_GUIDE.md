# How to Use Penny: A Simple Guide for Everyone

Welcome to **Penny**! 

Penny is like a digital version of the classic "paper envelope" budgeting method. It helps you keep track of your cash, bank accounts, and monthly bills—all kept **100% private and safe right on your own phone.**

You don't need any tech skills to master this app. Here is everything you need to know in simple terms!

---

## 🔑 Step 1: Setting Up on Your First Launch

When you first open the app, you will do two quick setup steps:

1. **Accept the Privacy Agreement:** Penny is offline-first. This agreement simply promises that your budget numbers stay strictly on your phone. Nobody else can see them! Tap **"I Accept and Agree"** to open the app.
2. **Create Your 6-Digit Lock PIN:** Pick 6 numbers that you will easily remember. This PIN protects your app so that if someone else holds your phone, they can't open Penny and look at your cash ledger!

---

## 💰 Step 2: Labeled Envelopes & Wallets

Think of Penny as having physical paper envelopes and different physical wallets.

### 💼 Your Wallets & Accounts
* These are the actual places you keep your money (like GCash, Bank accounts, or cash in your pocket).
* To add one, tap **"Add Account"** under the wallet list, type a name (like "My GCash"), and type how much money is currently inside.

### ✉️ Labeled Budget Envelopes
* These are your monthly allowances (like Food, Rent, or Fuel).
* **Smart Icons:** You don't need to pick pictures! If you name your envelope **"Food"**, Penny automatically draws a little spoon and fork. If you name it **"Rent"**, Penny draws a house!
* **Ready to Assign:** This is income you haven't assigned to an envelope yet. Tap **"Assign"** to put this money into your envelopes!

---

## 💸 Step 3: Recording a Spending

When you buy food or pay for fuel, recording it takes just 5 seconds:

1. Tap the big green **`+` (Plus)** button at the bottom right.
2. Type in the amount (e.g., `150`).
3. Select which envelope you spent it from (e.g., "Food").
4. Tap **Save**! The app instantly subtracts it from that envelope.

### 🔴 What is the Red Bar?
* Every envelope has a thin progress line. 
* If you have spent too much and went past your envelope budget, the line turns **bright red** and says **"OVERSPENT"**. This is your quick warning to slow down spending in that category!

---

## 📊 Step 4: Seeing Where Your Money Went

Tap the **Analytics (Chart)** tab at the bottom to see your progress:
* You will see a beautiful colored ring showing your spending.
* Want to know how much you spent on Food? Simply tap the Food slice on the ring, or tap "Food" in the list below it. The screen will instantly display the exact amount!
* Tap the highlighted center summary card at any time to reset and view your total spending.

---

## 🔔 Step 5: Automatic Helpful Reminders

Penny helps you stay disciplined without you having to configure anything:
* **8:00 PM Daily Check:** The app will send you a friendly message every evening to remind you to log your transactions for the day.
* **Due Date Reminders:** When you add a recurring monthly bill, Penny will send a reminder exactly **1 day before** the bill is due at **9:00 AM** so you never get hit with late fees!

---

### 🛡️ Why You Can Trust Penny
Your budget data belongs strictly to you. Penny does not link to your bank accounts, requires no credit cards, and **never uploads your data to the internet**. Everything is stored safely inside your physical phone!

*Happy budgeting!*
